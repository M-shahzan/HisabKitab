## 1. Evaluation Protocol & Ethics

- **Objective:** Evaluate usability, speech comfort, language naturalness, and trust in voice billing among local merchants.
- **Informed Consent:** Explain that this is a student hackathon research project. Obtain verbal consent before asking questions or noting verbatim quotes. Do not record audio without explicit permission.
- **Privacy:** Store anonymized participant identifiers (e.g., *Shopkeeper 1 — Malleshwaram Vegetable Stall*). Do not record personal identification or financial numbers.
